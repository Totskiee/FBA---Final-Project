Company Background & Business Problem

How TitanMotor Logistics Corp. turned a 24.8% logistics overhead crisis into an optimized, data-driven supply chain competitive advantage.

TitanMotor Logistics Corp.

TitanMotor is a nationwide manufacturer of high-torque commercial and industrial electric motors (3-Phase Induction, DC Brushless, Heavy Servo Motors, Explosion-Proof drives). The company operates 3 primary production warehouses (Chicago, Dallas, Philadelphia) supplying 5 major regional assembly hubs across North America.

Heavy unit cargo (45 kg - 600+ kg)

The Cost Dilemma

Due to volatile carrier fuel rates, uncoordinated warehouse dispatching, and arbitrary carrier selection, quarterly freight shipping costs spiked by 24.8%, exceeding \$145,000 monthly. Suboptimal route matching also led to delivery bottlenecks at client automotive and energy assembly facilities.

Unsustainable freight overhead

The Business Analytics Solution

The company deployed a cohesive full-stack analytics system that unifies 5 relational database entities. The system connects Descriptive KPIs, Predictive Regression for price & transit forecasting, and Prescriptive Linear Programming to compute the lowest-cost shipping schedule.

$13,680+ verified cost reduction

Step-Wise Methodology

The 3-Tier Business Analytics Pipeline

Connected sequentially from historical observations to actionable mathematical prescriptions.

Step 01 What Happened?

Descriptive Analytics

Aggregates 120 historical transactions to reveal freight cost disparities, carrier volume concentration, lane cost per ton-km, and delivery bottlenecks.

  • Spend by Destination Hubs
  • Carrier Contract Distribution
  • Carrier Transit & Delay Analysis
Step 02 What Will Happen?

Predictive Analytics

Trains a multi-variable regression model on historical shipment data to forecast freight costs and expected transit days before dispatching cargo.

  • Interactive "What-If" Calculator
  • High Fit Accuracy ($R^2 = 0.924$)
  • Dynamic Cargo Weight Calculations
Step 03 What Should We Do?

Prescriptive Analytics

Solves the Transportation Problem Linear Program to compute the exact optimal shipping schedule from warehouses to customer hubs that minimizes total cost.

  • Optimal Distribution Matrix
  • Actionable Route Directives
  • $13,680 (16.2%) Net Cost Savings
